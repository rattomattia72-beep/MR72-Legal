This Privacy Policy explains how HIT 72, published under the MR72 brand, handles information when you use the game.

2. Information stored locally

HIT 72 may store game information locally on your device, including your best score, settings, privacy notice status and whether the AD-FREE entitlement is active. This information is used to operate the game and provide its features.

3. Advertising

HIT 72 uses Google AdMob to provide advertising, including interstitial and rewarded ads.

Depending on your location, privacy choices and device settings, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device information, IP address, advertising identifiers where permitted, approximate location derived from network information, advertising interactions and app usage information.

For users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland, HIT 72 uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent where required.

You can review or change eligible advertising privacy choices from the Privacy Choices section inside HIT 72.

4. App Tracking Transparency

On Apple devices, HIT 72 may request App Tracking Transparency permission when required for advertising or measurement features. Refusing tracking permission does not prevent you from playing HIT 72.

5. In-App Purchases

HIT 72 offers an optional non-consumable AD-FREE purchase. Purchases are processed by the platform store, such as Apple's App Store. MR72 does not receive your full payment card details.

The app may receive information necessary to verify whether the purchase entitlement is active.

6. Third-party services

HIT 72 uses third-party services that may process information under their own privacy policies, including:

  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform
  • Apple App Store, StoreKit and TestFlight on Apple platforms

7. Data retention

Game data stored locally remains on your device until it is removed by the app, reset, or deleted when you uninstall the app, subject to platform behaviour.

Information processed by third-party services is retained according to those providers' own policies and legal obligations.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or withdrawal of consent.

For advertising consent managed through Google UMP, available choices can be reviewed through the Privacy Choices feature in HIT 72.

For privacy questions relating directly to MR72, contact info.mr72brand@gmail.com.

9. Children's privacy

HIT 72 is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Where local law requires parental or guardian consent for data processing, applicable platform and advertising requirements must be followed.

10. Security

Reasonable measures are used to reduce risks to information handled directly by the app. No system or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when HIT 72 features, advertising systems, legal requirements or third-party services change. The current version will remain available at this page.
