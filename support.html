HIT 72 Support

Official help and contact information for HIT 72, published under the MR72 brand.

Need help with gameplay, technical issues, advertisements or in-app purchases? Check the answers below or contact our support team.

How do I play HIT 72?

Tap at the right moment to hit the target. Improve your timing, build your score and try to reach 72.

What happens when I reach 72?

72 is only the beginning. Keep playing to discover what comes next.

What are rewarded ads?

Rewarded advertisements are optional. When available, you can choose to watch one to continue a run. Availability may vary.

What does Ad-Free Mode include?

Ad-Free Mode is an optional, one-time in-app purchase. It removes automatic interstitial advertisements and provides one free Continue per run. Optional rewarded advertisements are separate from automatic interstitial ads.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Ad-Free Mode panel in HIT 72 and select Restore Purchase. Make sure you are using the Apple Account associated with the original purchase. If your purchase does not appear, contact us with the details below. Please do not send passwords or payment card information.

How do I manage advertising privacy choices?

Where available, use the Privacy Choices option inside HIT 72. You can also manage app permissions in your device settings. See our Privacy Policy for more information.

How can I report a problem?

Send us a short description of the issue, your device model, iOS version and the app version. A screenshot can be helpful. Please avoid including sensitive information.
